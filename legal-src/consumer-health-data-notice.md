# Consumer Health Data Notice

**Version 1.0 · Effective 18 October 2026**

This notice is for residents of Washington State (My Health My Data Act), Nevada (SB 370), Connecticut and other US states with consumer health data laws. It applies alongside our [Privacy Policy](https://exomudra.com/privacy), and in a conflict this notice wins for consumer health data.

It's provided by **Exomudra Technologies Private Limited**, Flat 4, East Square Apartment, 18/30 V.O.C Nagar Main Road, Anna Nagar East, Perambur Purasawalkam, Chennai 600102, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: contact@exomudra.com.

Exomudra is a general wellness and exercise product. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition.

## 1. What consumer health data we collect

Only from signed-in users, and only with consent:

- **Exercise results from Hand Rehab:** ranges of finger movement you reach, your starting level and levels played, session length, repetitions and success rates.
- **Your check-ins:** comfort, pain (0 to 10) and fatigue ratings after sessions, and the goals you write in your own words.
- **Calibration measurements** saved with your exercise data (for example, palm length).
- **Recorded gesture samples**, if you back them up: short sequences of hand landmark points (not video), which some laws treat as biometric data.
- **Screen recordings of Rehab and ASL screens**, if you allow them. All text on these screens is hidden and the camera and 3D views are never recorded, but the fact that you used Hand Rehab, and how you moved through it, could reveal health-related information.
- **Rehab usage events**, only if you have agreed to both "Share usage data" and "Store my exercise results in my Exomudra account": for example, "level 12 started", "rep succeeded". Without both, Rehab sends only navigation events such as "screen opened".

We do not collect camera video. Hand tracking runs on your computer and camera images are never uploaded.

## 2. Why we collect it

- To show you your own progress over time and sync it to your other computers.
- To set the difficulty of your exercises and games.
- To lower difficulty and show a stop message when you report pain.
- To recognise the gestures you trained (gesture samples).
- To understand where people struggle in the app and improve it (usage events and recordings, only if you opt in).

We don't use consumer health data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

## 3. Where it comes from

Directly from you and your use of the app: your camera or Exomudra glove (processed on your computer into numbers), your answers, and your activity in the app. We don't buy health data or get it from other companies.

## 4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that process it for us under contract:

- **Supabase** (database and storage, Frankfurt, Germany): exercise data and gesture samples.
- **PostHog** (analytics, EU Cloud, Frankfurt): Rehab usage events and Rehab and ASL screen recordings, only if you opt in.

We share it with nobody else, except where the law requires (for example, a valid court order) or to protect someone's life or safety. We'll publish any change to this list here before it takes effect.

## 5. We don't sell consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data, and we will not do so.

## 6. Consent

We collect consumer health data only after you agree, through separate choices in the app:

- **"Store my exercise results in my Exomudra account"** (shown the first time you open Hand Rehab). If you don't agree, Hand Rehab keeps results only on your computer.
- **"Share usage data"** together with "Store my exercise results": both are needed before any Rehab performance or pain event is sent to our analytics provider.
- **"Include Rehab screens in usage recordings"** (the same choice covers ASL Practice screens; it appears in Settings → Privacy as "Rehab and ASL recordings").
- **"Back up my gesture recordings to my account"**, which also serves as your written release for biometric data.

Each consent is separate, optional for using the app, and can be withdrawn at any time in Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing stops collection immediately. If you withdraw "Store my exercise results", we offer you an export first, then delete the exercise data in your account after you confirm.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it, including a list of the third parties and affiliates we shared it with;
- withdraw your consent;
- have your consumer health data deleted. We delete it from our systems and tell our service providers to delete it too, including from archives and backups within the time the law allows.

**How to exercise them:**
- **In the app:** Settings → Privacy for your consents, "Export my data", "Delete my analytics data" and "Clear local data", and Settings → Account → "Delete my account".
- **By email:** contact@exomudra.com, from the email address on your account.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to another 45 days), we'll tell you why. These requests are free (up to twice a year).

**Appeals:** if we decline your request, reply to our email with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll respond within 45 days, explaining our decision. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or your own state's Attorney General.

## 8. Retention

- Exercise data: until you delete it, withdraw consent, or delete your account.
- Gesture samples: until you delete them, delete your account, or 3 years after your last sign-in, whichever comes first.
- Rehab and ASL screen recordings: 30 days.
- Usage events: kept only as long as we need them to improve the app, and you can delete yours at any time with "Delete my analytics data" in Settings → Privacy. We have not yet confirmed a fixed automatic deletion period for events with our analytics provider, so we do not state one; we will add it here when we do.

## 9. Security

Consumer health data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database rules mean only you can read your exercise data and gesture samples. Only Exomudra staff who need access to run the service can reach it, and they're bound to confidentiality.

## 10. Geofencing

We don't use location to target people near health care facilities, and we don't collect precise location at all.
