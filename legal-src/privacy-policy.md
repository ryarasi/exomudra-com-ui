## 2. The short version

- **Your camera stays on your computer.** Hand tracking runs locally. Camera video is never uploaded, stored or recorded by us, and the camera and 3D views are never included in screen recordings.
- **You can use the app without an account.** Guests give us no name or email.
- **Analytics and screen recordings are off until you turn them on**, and you can turn them off at any time.
- **Exercise results are only stored in your account if you say yes**, separately from everything else.
- **Recorded gesture samples are only uploaded if you say yes.** Otherwise they stay on your computer.
- **We don't sell your data**, and we don't use it for advertising.
- **Accounts are for people 18 and over.**

### 3.2 Content you create

Gesture profiles (bindings, gesture definitions, titles, notes), custom vocabularies, likes and reports, and app progress (levels, XP, streak tokens, signs you've learned). We store these to run the features you use. Legal basis: contract.

Profiles you make **public** or **unlisted** can be seen by others. Public profiles show your display name. Their bindings and notes are licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see the Terms). If a public profile includes your own custom gestures, their hand-position templates (normalised numbers, not images) are visible too; you agree to that with a separate checkbox when you publish, and they may be used only inside Exomudra. If you delete your account, your profiles are deleted and your custom gesture templates are removed from every copy other people made. Public profiles never contain your raw gesture samples.

### 3.3 Hand-tracking data

The app turns camera images (or glove readings) into hand positions and joint angles **on your computer**.

- **Camera images:** processed in memory and thrown away. Never stored, uploaded or recorded.
- **Joint angles and hand landmarks:** used live by the mini-apps. Never sent to our analytics.
- **Calibration** (for example, your palm length, used to estimate how far your hand is from the camera): stored on your computer. If you're signed in and use Hand Rehab with saving on, it goes in your account as part of your exercise data.

### 3.4 Recorded gesture samples (optional upload)

When you train a custom gesture or sign, the app records a few short sequences of hand landmark points (numbers, not video). These stay on your computer unless you turn on **"Back up my gesture recordings to my account"**, which asks for your written consent first.

Some laws, including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act and the Texas biometric law, may treat hand geometry as a biometric identifier. We don't use these samples to identify you. We use them only to recognise your gestures and to sync them to your other computers. If you upload them:

- we keep them until you delete them, delete your account, or 3 years after your last sign-in, whichever comes first, and then destroy them;
- we never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from them, and never disclose them except to our storage provider (Supabase) to provide the service, or where the law requires. The one exception is "Contributing your signs" below, which is a separate choice you'd have to make, and which we don't offer to residents of Illinois or Texas.

Legal basis: explicit consent.

**Contributing your signs (optional, separate).** ASL Practice may later offer "Contribute your signs", which uses your recordings to train our sign recogniser for everyone. That is a separate choice with its own consent screen, for adults only. It isn't offered to people who tell us they live in Illinois or Texas. You can withdraw at any time: we then delete your recordings and leave them out of future training. Without that choice, your samples are used only for your own gestures.

### 3.5 Exercise data (Hand Rehab, optional)

If you choose **"Store my exercise results in my Exomudra account"**, we store your exercise results: ranges of movement you reach, levels, session length, repetitions, success rates, and the comfort, pain and fatigue check-ins you answer, plus your goals in your own words.

This can be health-related data. We collect it only with your separate, explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), and use it only to show you your progress and set your exercise difficulty. If you don't consent, Hand Rehab still works and keeps results only on your computer. Exomudra is a wellness product, not a medical device, and these results are not medical measurements.

### 3.6 Usage analytics (optional)

If you turn on **"Share usage data"**, the app sends us events about how you use it: which screens you open, what you tap, which levels you play, whether a gesture or sign was recognised, app version, operating system, whether a glove is connected, and similar. For signed-in users these are linked to a random account ID, not your email. For guests they're linked to a random ID stored on your computer.

Events never contain camera images, joint angles, hand landmarks or gesture samples. Exercise events contain only summaries (for example, "level 12, rep succeeded, held 800 ms").

We keep usage events only as long as we need them to improve the app, and you can delete yours at any time with "Delete my analytics data" in Settings → Privacy. We have not yet confirmed a fixed automatic deletion period for events with our analytics provider, so we do not state one; we will add it here when we do.

Legal basis: consent.

### 3.7 Screen recordings (optional, signed-in users only)

If you turn on **"Allow screen recordings"**, we record how you move through the app window so we can see where people get stuck. These are reconstructions of the app's interface, not video of you.

- Your camera preview and all 3D or game views are never recorded. They show as grey blocks.
- Everything you type is hidden.
- On Hand Rehab and ASL Practice screens, all text is hidden as well (so your numbers and answers are not visible), and recordings only happen if you also turned on "Include Rehab screens in usage recordings". That one choice covers both Hand Rehab and ASL Practice, and appears in Settings → Privacy as "Rehab and ASL recordings".
- Some steps, such as camera framing during calibration, are never recorded.
- Recordings never run for guests.

Recordings are kept for 30 days and then deleted. Legal basis: consent (explicit consent for Rehab and ASL screens).

### 3.8 Technical and security data

When you sign in, download the app or check for updates, our providers receive your IP address and basic device information. We use this to deliver codes and downloads, prevent abuse (for example, rate-limiting sign-in codes) and keep the service secure. Our analytics project has IP address capture turned off. Legal basis: legitimate interest and contract.

### 3.9 Website (exomudra.com)

The website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. If you join the waitlist, we collect your email to tell you about launches (consent). The waitlist form is currently hosted on Google Forms, so Google processes that email for us.

## 7. Your choices and rights

**In the app, any time:** Settings → Privacy lets you turn on or off "Share usage data", "Allow screen recordings", "Rehab and ASL recordings", "Store my exercise results" and "Back up my gesture recordings to my account". It also has **Export my data**, **Delete my analytics data** (deletes your analytics history) and **Clear local data**. Settings → Account lets you **delete your account**. Turning a toggle off stops collection straight away.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you can:

- get a copy of your data and know how it's used (access and portability);
- correct or complete it;
- delete it;
- withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what we did before);
- object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interest;
- under the DPDP Act, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated;
- complain to a regulator.

To use any right, use the in-app tools or email contact@exomudra.com. We'll reply within 30 days and may need to confirm the request comes from the account's email address.

**Complaints.** Contact our Grievance Officer first (contact@exomudra.com). If you're not satisfied:
- in India, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India;
- in the EU or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

### 7.1 California and other US states

In the past 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (email, account ID), personal characteristics (birth year and month), internet and app activity (analytics, if enabled), geolocation at country level, inferences (difficulty level), and sensitive personal information (health-related exercise data and possibly biometric gesture samples, only with consent).

- We **do not sell or share** personal information, as the CCPA defines those terms, and haven't in the past 12 months.
- We use **sensitive personal information** only to provide the features you asked for. You can still ask us to limit its use: email contact@exomudra.com with "Limit the use of my sensitive personal information", or turn off exercise-data saving and gesture backups in the app.
- You can access, correct and delete your data, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. An authorised agent can make a request for you with your signed permission.
- Our website honours Global Privacy Control signals, although it has no tracking to switch off.

Residents of other US states with similar laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and Oregon) have similar rights, and can exercise them in the same way. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our refusal email, and we'll respond within 60 days.

## 8. Children

Accounts are only for people 18 and over. We ask for your birth year and month at sign-up and don't create accounts for anyone younger. Guests of any age can use the free features without giving us personal data, but guests never get screen recordings, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email contact@exomudra.com and we'll delete it.

## 9. Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest with our providers. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your computer using your operating system's keychain. Database access is restricted so each user can only reach their own private data. Each mini-app is isolated and only gets the data its permissions allow. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we'll notify affected users and the authorities as the law requires (in India, the Data Protection Board within 72 hours of becoming aware; in the EU, the relevant authority within 72 hours where required).
