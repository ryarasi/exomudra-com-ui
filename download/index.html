Opening it the first time

This preview build isn't signed yet, so your computer will warn you the first time. That's expected.

macOS (Apple silicon, M1 or later)

  1. Open the downloaded installer. macOS blocks it.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway within an hour of the block.
  4. Enter your password.

Windows

  1. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose Keep, then Keep anyway.
  2. On "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Windows 11 with Smart App Control on: it blocks unsigned apps with no override. Turn it off in Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control, or wait for the signed release.

In the app: try the Playground free for 10 minutes, then sign in with your email (no password) to keep going.
